cd competitor-campaign-intelligence
python3 app.py

From public ads to a useful finding

A repeatable research workflow, with a source behind every observation.

01

Collect a baseline

Capture ad IDs, copy, source links and available destinations for the same five advertisers. Save the collection time and scope.

02

Compare observations

Compare later snapshots in the same scope. Identify copy and offer changes, new observations and meaningful landing-page text changes.

03

Inspect the evidence

Read the before and after copy and open the public source. A rule-based label helps triage; your judgment decides what is useful.

04

Record a decision

Mark findings useful, not useful or acted on. Compare research time and cost with a manual review to evaluate the workflow.

What the evidence can tell us

  • First observed means first seen in these saved collections. It is not a launch date.
  • Audience language in copy is an interpretation. Disclosed targeting, when available, is displayed separately.
  • Not observed is used only for comparable complete collections. It never proves a campaign stopped.
  • Public ads do not reveal conversions, revenue, ROAS or whether a campaign works.
  • Landing pages can change by location, personalization or an A/B test. Review a change before calling it a strategy shift.
